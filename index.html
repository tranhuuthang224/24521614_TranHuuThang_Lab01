Live Defense: Keybinding Contract Rebinding

Architecture decouples physical keys from audio synthesis. Key bindings reside exclusively in HTML data-key and data-code attributes.

  1. Step 1: Locate target pad in index.html (e.g. <button class="drum-pad" data-sound="kick">).
  2. Step 2: Change data-key="x", data-code="KeyX", and <kbd class="key-label">X</kbd>.
  3. Step 3: Save and reload. The input controller queries the DOM contract dynamically — Zero JS refactor required (< 30 seconds).
